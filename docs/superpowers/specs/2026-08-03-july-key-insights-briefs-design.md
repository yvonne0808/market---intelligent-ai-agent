# July Key Insights Briefs Design

## Goal

Create two independent, readable three-page executive briefs from the current
July reports: one for Pharma and one for Medical Device.

## Scope

- Preserve all current monthly reports, public pages, and PDFs unchanged.
- Reuse only current analyzed report data and original article links.
- Produce a separate three-page brief for each domain.
- Do not collect articles or call the LLM unless later explicitly approved.

## Content Structure

Each brief has three pages:

1. **Five Key Takeaways** — concise business conclusions, emphasizing direct
   packaging signals, commercial launch or volume, capacity, supply chain, and
   customer/product developments.
2. **Top Opportunities** — four to six card-style evidence items: company and
   date, event, why it matters, and one original-source link.
3. **Watchlist and References** — follow-up companies/events plus eight to
   twelve specific original links.

Pure policy and regulation items are background only. They do not use a key
takeaway or top-opportunity slot unless the underlying article explicitly
states a packaging, production, volume, procurement, or customer consequence.

## Output

Create separately named static HTML and card-style PDF briefs under the public
July Pharma and Medical Device report directories, with visible links from
their report pages or the homepage. Validate page count, layout, source links,
and public URLs before publication.
